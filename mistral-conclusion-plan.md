### What conflicts with the brief, and what I did

1. **"Le Chat, not Vibe."** Mistral's own pricing page now sells the assistant as **Vibe** and mentions "Le Chat" once, in brackets. The video says "Mistral's free assistant" and avoids the name.
2. **Running Le Chonk locally.** Its Hugging Face name, 1T-A52B, means 1 trillion parameters with 52 billion active. That needs data-centre GPUs, not a laptop. The video says so plainly and points classmates to the small open models instead.
3. **"Commercial use is allowed."** True with one condition: a company selling the model itself as a service needs a commercial licence. The video says "free to use and build on" and leaves out the commercial claim.
4. **Mistral Small 4** (Mar 2026) is open (Apache 2.0) but has 119B parameters, too big for most laptops, so the video uses Small 3.1 for "on your laptop".
5. **Left out:** benchmark claims ("best open-weight model for coding"), revenue and compute figures.

## 2. Script, version 2 (about 90 seconds, read on screen, final line in French)

Change from version 1: the ending now leads into Session 10's exercise, "Redesign campaign workflow". It shows the professor's six steps, lights up the two that Alan's deck already gave to machines, and ends on his questions without answering them.

1. France built its own frontier AI. On 29 October, Mistral opened the weights of Le Chonk, a 1-trillion-parameter model.
2. Anyone can download it, inspect it and build on it. Mistral says it was trained from scratch in Europe.
3. That's sovereignty: owning your AI instead of renting it from abroad. Your data, your model, your servers.
4. Investors are betting on it: €3 billion raised in September, at a valuation above €21 billion.
5. An honest note: a trillion parameters needs a data centre. Smaller Mistral models fit on a laptop.
6. Try this week, 1: Mistral's free assistant, on web and mobile.
7. Try this week, 2: run a model offline. Mistral says Small 3.1 runs on a Mac with 32 GB of RAM.
8. Try this week, 3: Mistral Vibe, an open-source AI agent on GitHub.
9. Next step: agents. Mistral's Studio builds agents that plan and act inside a business.
10. Here is a campaign workflow today: brief, creative, legal review, media buying, launch, analytics.
11. In Alan's deck, two of these steps were already machines: the AI writer did the creative, the AI buyer did the media.
12. Session 10 asks: what if agents ran the whole chain? New opportunities? And what is the human role?
13. *La souveraineté, c'est aussi garder un humain dans la boucle.* (Sovereignty also means keeping a human in the loop.)

About 170 words in 90 seconds, readable as captions.
